📌 Overlay URL (Stable)

Stable Overlay URL

🚀 Quick Setup (3 Steps)

Step 1: Open Prism Live

Start your Prism Live Streaming App and create/open a scene.

Step 2: Add Browser Source

Click Add Source → Web Page
Paste the overlay URL above
Set dimensions: 1920 × 1080 (or your stream resolution)

Step 3: Control & Broadcast

Open the Control Panel in your browser: https://kanha360.github.io/news-flash-syetem/
Create messages, customize settings, and click "Start Telecast"

✓ The overlay will appear in Prism Live and update in real-time!

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Separate Monitor: Keep the control panel on a different screen
  • Test First: Preview messages before going live
  • Good Contrast: Use colors that stand out from your background
  • Strategic Position: Use "Upper", "Lower", or "Center" positioning
  • Responsive URL: This URL is stable and won't change

🎨 Customization

In the Control Panel, you can customize:

  • 📝 Flash Message Text
  • 🎨 Font & Background Colors
  • 📍 Text Alignment (Left, Center, Right)
  • 📺 Overlay Position (Upper, Lower, Center, Full Screen)
  • ✍️ Font Family & Size
  • ⏱️ Display Duration
